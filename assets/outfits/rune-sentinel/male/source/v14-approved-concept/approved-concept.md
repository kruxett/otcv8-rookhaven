# Rune Sentinel — approved design direction

The user approved the concept and development of a Rune Sentinel elite guard from an ancient rune order. This does not approve a particular sprite or game integration.

## Complete base costume, before addons
- Fitted open-face plate helmet, simple brow band and angular side plates. Preserve native face exposure and head placement.
- A broad coherent breastplate with one inset runestone as the main focal point. Large clean planes, deliberate highlights, no noisy chainmail substitute.
- A substantial belt and two overlapping short hip plates. Separate legs remain visible.
- Dark fitted undergarment, plain integral shoulder caps, metal bracers.
- Shaped knee/shin armor and armored boots following the original joints and toe positions.
- Repeat the angular plate construction across the body; concentrate ornament at the chest and later addons.

## Independent addons, later
1. Ceremonial helmet: low crest and larger inset runestone.
2. Ceremonial armor: paired overlapping shoulder plates and worked collar, visibly attached. Also works without addon 1. No flag.

## Art and anatomy contract
Citizen supplies actual anatomical placement and pose, not the garment design. Preserve face, hands, head/arm/leg/foot locations and proportions. Repaint the entire clothing surface, including old shirt folds: freezing them produced the rejected wrinkled-Citizen result. Paint at native scale, no downsampling. Neutral grayscale recolorable artwork with separate native four-channel masks; fixed skin retains its exact colors. Review south base first, then the same design in all directions and walk phases. No sprite is approved merely because a geometric or alpha check passes.
