# Original Rookhaven Keyring shop artwork

User-approved original Keyring spell illustration, generated directly at 32x32
in PixelLab Pro Flash. Three collected-key symbols use shaded brass/steel,
dark contours and the material rendering of Rookhaven's existing shop art.
The approved Magic Quiver sprite guided style only; its design was not copied.

`source/pixellab-keyring-style.png` is the untouched approved result; `sprite.png`
and `modules/game_shop/images/keyring.png` are byte-identical copies. `preview.png`
is an 8x nearest-neighbour enlargement for inspection only. Provenance, generation
ID and SHA256 are recorded in `source/generation.json`.

The final native image has binary transparency, visible bounds (2,1)-(29,28)
and no clipped edge pixels. It is a static shop illustration, not a new physical
item. No SID/CID, DAT/SPR/OTB records, price or Keychain spell grants change.

Keyring teaches Consume, Unlock and List: newly collected keys are consumed
and permanently remembered per character; the matching key can operate the
ordinary door in front of the character. This illustration represents that
ability bundle. Earlier unselected designs remain local concept work;
`source/dev10107-generation.json` records the preceding released artwork.
